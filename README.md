# [WaveSource](https://source.toldyouso.com)
- When matter becomes optional. Download the frequency. Experience the effect.

WaveSource is a platform for capturing frequency signatures from physical materials and short-lived phenomena, then turning those recordings into shareable wave bundles.

Note: this page is here to help with the rush in traffic from Hacker News, Slashdot and other sources. If the forum is a bit slow, that's why. Thanks for being patient.

I built this out of practical frustration. A lot of the things I wanted to work with are rare, hard to source in pure form, or extremely time-sensitive. Fresh coconut water is a good example — it changes fast, the useful window is short, and once that moment is gone you can’t really get it back. The same goes for certain plants, environmental conditions, fields and other phenomena that only exist in a particular state for a limited time. I wanted a way to record what was actually present instead of just losing it.

The capture process uses resonance methods. Samples are read and recorded with unique antennas. Some of the early results were more consistent and more surprising than I expected. That opened up a can of delicious worms and a world of new possibilities I am still trying to wrap my head around. Different categories of these captures are being organized into WaveVaults. See the [forum](https://source.toldyouso.com) for more.

People have been testing them in food, supplements, performance work, nootropics, recovery, and some longer-term vitality experiments. All of this is still early and experimental. The effects aren’t uniform, I don’t claim to fully understand the mechanisms yet, and I’m approaching the whole thing as an ongoing investigation with all of you more than a finished product.
